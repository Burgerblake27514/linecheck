Notes

What actually changed

The Beer-Cheese Pretzel jump is real, not seasonal noise — it stayed the top mover after backing out one large private event, and it's now the single best-selling appetizer in the house.

Ribeye and Harvest Salad both slipped double digits — worth a look at whether that's a menu placement issue, a price point issue, or just July's lighter dinner crowd. Nothing in the numbers alone answers that, but it's the first place to ask the floor staff.

Delivery grew slower than in-house — up 6.1% against 9.9% overall. Not a problem on its own, but worth watching if it continues for a second month.

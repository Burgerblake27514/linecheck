What's in the report

One PDF, every month

Category rollup

Every category with units, net sales, share of total, and change against last month. Appetizers up, salads down, in one place.

Top sellers, with rank change

Your top ten by net sales — and whether each one climbed or slipped since last month. Rank movement is where the story usually is.

Biggest movers

Items that jumped or dropped the most, filtered to real volume so a single catering order doesn't top the list.

Delivery kept separate

Third-party delivery orders skew toward a narrow set of items at different prices. Mixed in, they make your menu look like it's shifting when it isn't. We report both, separately.

Price and mix effects

Average item price alongside unit volume, so you can tell a real sales increase from a price increase.

Plain-language notes

A short written read on what actually changed and what's worth a look — not just a wall of numbers.

Questions

Which POS systems work?

Any system that can export a product mix or item sales report as a spreadsheet or CSV — which is nearly all of them. If yours exports something unusual, send a sample and we'll tell you honestly whether it works before you pay anything.

Do I need to connect Line Check to my POS?

No. You upload a file you export yourself. Line Check never connects to your POS, your bank, or your payment processing, and never sees customer or payment data.

How do I cancel?

Through a self-service billing portal, any time, without contacting us. Your subscription runs to the end of the period you've paid for and then stops. There's no contract and no cancellation fee.

What's your refund policy?

If a report doesn't arrive or isn't usable, that month is refunded — email and it's handled. The 14-day trial is there so you can see a real report built from your own data before any card is charged.

Is the setup fee required?

No. It's for restaurants that want their menu mapping, category structure, and first report handled for them rather than doing the initial setup themselves.

Who's behind this?

Line Check is run by Blake, an independent data analyst based in central Illinois. It's a small operation on purpose — you deal with the person who builds the reports.
